White Elephant Rules

The gift exchange where stealing is encouraged.

How to play White Elephant

  1. Everyone brings one wrapped, anonymous gift within the agreed budget.
  2. Gifts go in a pile. Everyone draws a number to set the turn order.
  3. Player 1 opens a gift from the pile.
  4. Each next player either opens a new gift or steals an already-opened gift.
  5. Anyone whose gift is stolen picks again: open a new one or steal from someone else (but not the gift just taken from them).
  6. The game ends when every gift is opened. Player 1 often gets a final chance to swap.

Common stealing rules

  • A gift can be stolen a maximum of 3 times. After that it's "frozen".
  • No immediate steal-backs.
  • Set a max number of steals per turn so the game keeps moving.

White Elephant vs. Secret Santa

White Elephant is a party game where gifts are random and often funny. In Secret Santa, each person buys a thoughtful gift for one specific person whose name was drawn. Many groups do both!
