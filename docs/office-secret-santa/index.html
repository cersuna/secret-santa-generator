How to Organize an Office Secret Santa

Run a stress-free work gift exchange in 10 minutes.

1. Make it opt-in

Send a quick message in Slack, Teams or email asking who wants to join. Never pressure coworkers. Some people can't afford it or don't celebrate.

2. Pick a modest budget

$10–$25 is standard at work. Choose a number everyone is comfortable with, then browse gift ideas under $20.

3. Collect wishlists

Ask people for 2–3 likes (coffee, plants, board games) and anything to avoid (allergies, alcohol). Use our Secret Santa questionnaire.

4. Draw names online

Paste the list into the DrawSanta generator, add the budget and party date, then DM each coworker their private link. No accounts or work emails needed, and HR won't have to manage a spreadsheet.

5. Include remote teammates

Remote coworkers can mail gifts directly or send a digital gift. Schedule a short video call for the unwrapping.

Office-appropriate gift rules

  • Keep it work-safe: no gag gifts that could embarrass anyone.
  • Avoid alcohol unless you know it's welcome.
  • Consumables, desk upgrades and cozy items are always safe.
