    How to draw names for Secret Santa online

    1. Add everyone's names. Type them in, or paste a whole list from a group chat. You can add a wishlist or hints for each person.
    2. Set the budget and date. These appear on every reveal so nobody forgets the spending limit.
    3. Add exclusions so partners or housemates can't draw each other.
    4. Click "Draw names", then send each person their private link by text, WhatsApp, email, Slack or Teams.
    5. Each person taps the gift box to unwrap their match and gets gift ideas that fit the budget.

    Why use DrawSanta instead of drawing from a hat?

    Pulling names from a hat only works if everyone is in the same room, and someone always draws their own name. An online Secret Santa generator fixes both problems. The draw is random, nobody gets themselves, and it works for families, offices and friends spread across different cities. Unlike most Secret Santa sites, DrawSanta doesn't make you create an account or collect everyone's email address. The links do the work.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this Secret Santa generator really free?

    Yes. DrawSanta is 100% free with no sign-up, no email addresses and no limit on how many people you add. Optional gift-idea links earn us a small affiliate commission at no cost to you.

    How does everyone find out who they got?

    After the draw, every participant gets their own private link. Send each person their link by text, WhatsApp, email or Slack. When they open it, they unwrap a gift box that shows who they're buying for, along with that person's wishlist and the budget.

    Will the organizer see who got who?

    No. The organizer only sees a list of names with a link next to each one, never the matches. That means the organizer can join in too. (Just don't open other people's links!)

    Can I stop couples or family members from drawing each other?

    Yes. Use Exclusions to stop specific people from drawing each other, either one-way or both ways. The generator also avoids two people drawing each other by default.

    Do you store our names?

    No. The draw runs entirely in your browser. Names live only in your browser and inside each private link. Nothing is uploaded to a server and no database exists.

    What if someone loses their link?

    The organizer's browser remembers the draw, so reopen this page on the same device and the links will still be there to copy again.

    Can I use it for an office or virtual Secret Santa?

    Absolutely. Because links can go through any messenger, DrawSanta works for office parties, remote teams, families spread across the country, classrooms and friend groups.

    How many people can join?

    There's no hard limit. Groups of 3 to 100+ work fine.
