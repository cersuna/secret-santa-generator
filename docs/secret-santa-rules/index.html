Secret Santa Rules

Everything you need to run a fair, fun gift exchange.

The basic rules

  1. Draw names randomly. Every participant is secretly assigned one other person. Nobody can draw themselves. Use a Secret Santa generator so it's instant and fair.
  2. Keep it secret. Don't tell anyone who you drew, especially not the person you're buying for.
  3. Agree on a budget. Everyone spends roughly the same amount, usually $20–$25.
  4. Set a deadline. Pick the date and place where gifts get exchanged.
  5. Buy one gift for your person, wrap it, and label it with their name (not yours).
  6. Exchange gifts. Gifts are handed out at the party, or mailed for remote groups.
  7. The reveal (optional). People guess their Santa, then Santas reveal themselves.

Optional rules that make it better

  • Wishlists: each person lists a few ideas or hobbies. DrawSanta shows these on each reveal.
  • Exclusions: couples and housemates shouldn't draw each other.
  • Themes: "something handmade", "a book", "something under $10 that's red".
  • No gift cards, or only gift cards. Decide up front.

Secret Santa etiquette

  • Don't go wildly over or under budget.
  • Avoid gag gifts at work unless everyone has agreed to them.
  • Thank your Santa, even if the gift isn't your style.
  • If you drop out, tell the organizer early so they can redraw.

Frequently asked questions

What is the point of Secret Santa?

Secret Santa lets a group exchange gifts without everyone buying for everyone. Each person buys one gift for one randomly assigned person, which keeps costs low and adds some mystery.

Do you reveal who your Secret Santa was?

It's up to your group. Some groups reveal at the party, some have everyone guess first, and some keep it secret forever.

What is a good Secret Santa budget?

$20–$25 is the most common budget. Offices often choose $10–$20, and families often go up to $50.

What's the difference between Secret Santa and White Elephant?

In Secret Santa each person buys for one assigned person. In White Elephant everyone brings one anonymous gift and players pick or steal gifts in turn. See White Elephant rules.
