Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Short version: DrawSanta doesn't have a database. Your names never leave your browser.

Secret Santa draws

Names, wishlists and draw results are processed in your browser. They're stored in your browser's local storage (so you can reopen your draw) and inside each private reveal link, after the # symbol, a part of the URL browsers never send to servers.
