Virtual Secret Santa

A long-distance gift exchange that still feels like Christmas.

How a virtual Secret Santa works

It's the same as a regular Secret Santa, except names are drawn online and gifts are shipped. The fun happens on a video call where everyone unwraps together.

Step by step

  1. Draw names online with the free DrawSanta generator. Send each person their link in your group chat or by DM.
  2. Share addresses privately. Add a note in the exchange details telling people to DM the organizer for their match's address, or ship to the organizer.
  3. Set a "ship by" date at least 7–10 days before the call. Online stores get slow in December.
  4. Order online and ship directly. Most stores offer gift wrap and gift receipts.
  5. Host the reveal call. Go around one by one: open the gift, guess your Santa, then the Santa reveals.
