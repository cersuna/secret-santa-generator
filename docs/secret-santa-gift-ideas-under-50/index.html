Tips for choosing a Secret Santa gift

  • Check their wishlist first. DrawSanta shows each person's wishlist on their reveal page.
  • Stick to the budget. Going far over can make others feel awkward.
  • Consumables are safe. Snacks, candles and coffee never clutter a home.
  • Include a gift receipt in case of sizing or duplicates.
  • Order early. Shipping slows down a lot in mid-December.
